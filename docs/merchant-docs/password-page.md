# Password page

While your store is password protected, visitors see the password page. It shows your logo (or store name), a message, an email signup form so visitors can hear when you open, and a form to enter the store password.

To turn password protection on or off, go to **Online Store > Preferences** in your Shopify admin. On a new store, protection stays on until you choose a paid plan.

In the theme editor, choose **Others > Password** in the page selector to edit the page.

## Password page section

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Eyebrow**, **Headline**, **Supporting line** | The text on the page, such as "Opening soon." |
| **Show password message** | Shows the message you write under **Online Store > Preferences > Password protection**. <!-- verify after merge --> |
| **Password link label** | The link that reveals the password form, such as "Enter using password." |
| **Password error** | The message shown when the password is wrong. |
| **Image** and **Image alt text** | An optional photo. |

Blocks:

- **Email signup** (1): **Label**, **Button** and **Success**. Signups are added to your customer list with email marketing consent, so you can email them when you open. <!-- verify after merge: currently "Email sign-up" -->
- **Social** (up to 4): a **Link**, **Network** (Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube or Facebook) and **Accessible label** (read by screen readers, such as "Instagram").
- **Message** (up to 2): a short **Text**, with **Show** and **Align** (**Center** or **Start**). <!-- verify after merge: "Centre" becomes "Center" -->

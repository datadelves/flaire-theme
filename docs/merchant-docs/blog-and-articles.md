# Blog and articles

Flaire presents your blog as a journal. Blog pages use the **Blog** template and articles use the **Article** template. Write and publish articles in your Shopify admin under **Content > Blog posts**.

## Blog page

### Journal hero

The top of the blog page.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Eyebrow**, **Headline**, **Supporting line** | The text at the top of the page. |
| **Show blog title as eyebrow** | On blog pages, shows the blog's name instead of the **Eyebrow** text. Useful when you have more than one blog. <!-- verify after merge --> |
| **Feature latest article** | Shows your newest article as a large feature. |

Blocks:

- **Topic link** (up to 6): links to article tags or other pages, such as "Care" or "Gifting." **Style** is **Secondary** (a button) or **Link**.
- **Featured article** (1): choose an **Article** to feature instead of the latest one, with an **Eyebrow** and **Excerpt**.
- **Note** (1): a short line of text.

### Article grid

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Articles per page** | From 6 to 24. Page links appear when there are more. |
| **Excerpt** | Shows each article's excerpt. If an article has no excerpt, the start of the article is used. |
| **Tag filter row** | Shows the blog's tags as filters above the grid. |
| **Author** | Shows the author on each card. |

Blocks:

- **Card options** (1): **Date**, **Read time** and **Image ratio** (**4:3** or **1:1**).
- **Inline promo** (up to 2): a promotional card in the grid, with a **Headline**, **Link** and **Image**.
- **Empty state** (1): the **Heading**, **Text** and **Link** shown when the blog has no articles. <!-- verify after merge: "Title" becomes "Heading" -->

Add each article's featured image and excerpt in the article editor in your Shopify admin. The image is used on cards, at the top of the article and when the article is shared on social media.

## Article page

### Article section

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Featured image** | Shows the article's image at the top. |
| **Author** | Shows the author's name. |
| **Date** | Shows the publish date. |
| **Tags** | Shows the article's tags as links. |
| **Show comments** | Shows comments and the comment form, when the blog allows comments. <!-- verify after merge: currently "Comments", off by default --> |
| **Table of contents** | Builds a list of links from the article's H2 headings. |

Blocks:

- **Title** (1): **Size** (**Headline 48** or **Display 64**), **Tag eyebrow** (shows the first tag above the heading) and **Align** (**Center** or **Start**). <!-- verify after merge: "Centre" becomes "Center"; block name may become "Heading" -->
- **Content** (1): **Body size**, **First paragraph as lead** (shows the first paragraph larger) and **Hairline under h2**.
- **Featured product** (up to 3): a product card inside the article, with an **Eyebrow** and **Quick view**.
- **Share** (1): **Label**, **Copy link** and **Native share**.
- App blocks.

Headings, quotes and lists in your article are styled the same way as in product and collection descriptions.

### Related articles

Shows other articles at the end of an article.

- **Articles**: how many, from 2 to 4.
- Blocks: **Pinned article** (up to 3; **Article**, **Heading override**, **Excerpt**), **Topic link** (up to 6) and **Link** (for example "All articles"). <!-- verify after merge: "Title override" becomes "Heading override" -->

## Comments

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Content > Blog posts**, select **Manage blogs**, and choose your blog.
2. Under **Comments**, choose **Comments are allowed, pending moderation** or **Comments are allowed**.
3. In the theme editor, turn on **Show comments** in the **Article** section. <!-- verify after merge -->

Comments are shown with page links when there are many. After a customer posts a comment, they see a message that it was posted, or that it will appear after you approve it. Moderate comments in your Shopify admin on the article's page.

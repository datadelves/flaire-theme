# Theme Store listing draft

## Theme name

Flaire

## Short description

A calm, photo-led theme for fine jewelry, with finish swatches, size guides, engraving and gift options built in.

## Long description

Flaire is made for jewelry stores that let their pieces speak. Large photography, quiet typography and generous white space keep the focus on the work, while the details customers look for before they buy are always close at hand.

Customers can compare finishes with swatches, check their ring or bracelet size in a size guide drawer, see a piece on scale, and add a short engraving before they add it to their bag. Clear delivery and pickup information, gift wrap notes and a gift card recipient form make it easy to buy for someone else.

Every page is built from sections and blocks, so you can tell your story your way: how pieces are made, what they are made of, and how to care for them. Filters from the Shopify Search & Discovery app, predictive search, quick view and a bag drawer help customers find and buy quickly on any device.

## Key features

- Finish swatches with built-in metal colors
- Size guide drawer for rings, bracelets and necklaces
- Engraving field with a live preview section
- On-scale product views and measurement sections
- Materials and stone guide sections
- Gift finder, gift notes and gift card recipient form
- Mega menu with metal, stone and help columns
- Quick view and quick add from product cards
- Product filtering and sorting
- Predictive search
- Bag drawer with recommendations and shipping estimates
- Local pickup availability
- Complementary and related product recommendations
- Recently viewed products
- Product videos, YouTube and Vimeo, and 3D models
- Stock counter and product badges
- Sticky header and sticky buy bar
- Image zoom
- Country, currency and language selectors
- Delivery cut-off message in the announcement bar

## Preset

### Flaire

A white canvas, near-black type, light gray bands and blue buttons give products the whole stage. The home page opens with a slideshow of cut-out pieces, then moves through collections, materials, engraving, craft, sizing, testimonials, the journal, questions and gifting. Suited to fine and demi-fine jewelry, engagement rings and wedding bands.

## Industries

- Jewelry and accessories (primary)
- Watches
- Luxury and gifts
- Bridal: engagement rings and wedding bands
- Small, high-value goods where material and size matter, such as eyewear and small leather goods

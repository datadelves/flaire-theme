# Pages, contact and 404

## Standard pages

Pages such as About, Shipping or Care use the **Default page** template. Write page content in your Shopify admin under **Online Store > Pages**.

### Page header

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Use page title** | Uses the page's name as the heading. Turn it off to write your own **Headline**. |
| **Eyebrow**, **Headline**, **Supporting line** | The text at the top of the page. |
| **Align** | **Center** or **Start**. <!-- verify after merge: "Centre" becomes "Center" --> |

Blocks: **Breadcrumb** (**Home**, **Aria label** for screen readers, **Compact**), **Image** (**Image**, **Image alt text**, **Width**: **Content** or **Wide**) and **Meta** (a line of **Text**, with **Updated date** to show when the page was last changed).

### Page content

Shows the content you wrote in the page editor.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Table of contents** | Builds a list of links from the page's H2 headings. Useful for long pages, such as a care guide. |
| **Width** | **Narrow 692** or **Content 980** pixels. |

Blocks:

- **Page content** (1): **Show title** (shows the page name above the content), **Body size** and **Hairlines under h2**.
- **Callout** (up to 4): a highlighted note with an **Icon**, **Heading** and **Text**. <!-- verify after merge: "Title" becomes "Heading" -->
- **Inline image** (up to 4): an **Image** with **Image alt text** and a **Caption**.

To create a different layout for some pages, select **Default page > Create template** in the theme editor's page selector, then choose the template on the page in your Shopify admin.

## Contact page

Flaire includes a contact template. To use it:

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store > Pages** and create a page, such as "Contact."
2. Under **Theme template**, choose **contact**.
3. Add the page to a menu, such as your footer menu.

Shopify emails each message to your store's contact email address.

### Contact form

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Eyebrow**, **Headline**, **Supporting line** | The text above the form. |
| **Button label** | The text on the send button. |
| **Success title** and **Success text** | The message shown after the form is sent. <!-- verify after merge: "Success title" may become "Success heading" --> |
| **Topic select** | Shows a drop-down where the customer chooses a topic. |

Blocks:

- **Field** (up to 8): a form field with a **Label**, a **Type** (**Text**, **Email**, **Phone** or **Message**) and **Required**. Keep one **Email** field so you can reply.
- **Topic option** (up to 8): an option in the topic drop-down, with a **Label**, a **Value** (included in the message you receive) and **Default**.
- **Store info** (up to 3): an **Icon**, **Heading** and **Address and hours** for a studio or store. <!-- verify after merge: "Title" becomes "Heading" -->

The supporting line in the preset says you reply within one working day. Change it to match how quickly you actually reply.

## 404 page

The 404 page appears when a customer follows a broken link. It uses the **404 page** template.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Headline** and **Supporting line** | The message, such as "This page has slipped away." |
| **Search field** | Shows a search field. |
| **Button label** and **Button link** | A button, for example to your home page. |

Blocks: **Category link** (up to 6 collections), **Help link** (up to 3) and **Featured product** (1).

To send customers from an old address to a new one, add a URL redirect in your Shopify admin under **Content > Menus > View URL redirects** (in some admins, **Online Store > Navigation**).

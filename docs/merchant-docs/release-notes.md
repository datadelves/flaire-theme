# Release notes

## 1.0.0

Release date: [RELEASE_DATE]

The first release of Flaire, a theme for fine-jewelry stores.

### Pages and sections

- Home page sections for jewelry stores: Hero slideshow, Hero tile, Shop by form, Featured product, Materials, plainly, Engraving studio, Craft in four steps, Size and scale, Testimonials, From the journal, Care and questions, Gift finder and Newsletter.
- Sections on every page, including product, collection, cart, blog, article and standard pages.
- Custom Liquid section and blocks, and app blocks in the header, footer, product, featured product, quick view, cart and other sections.
- Contact page with topic options and store information, and a 404 page with search and category links.

### Products

- Product page built from blocks: badge, title, lead, price, variant picker, engraving, purchase options, quantity, buy buttons, availability, gift note, collapsible rows and share.
- Finish swatches from Shopify's swatch colors and images, with built-in colors for common metals.
- Variant images shown when a customer chooses an option.
- Rich media: images, videos, YouTube and Vimeo videos, and 3D models with augmented reality on supported devices.
- Engraving field with a character limit, shown on products with a chosen tag.
- Gift card recipient form with name, email, message and send date.
- Shop Pay Installments message, unit prices, compare-at prices and a tax note.
- Accelerated checkout buttons on product pages, quick view and the featured product section.
- Local pickup availability with a pickup drawer.
- Size guide drawer with tabs for rings, bracelets and necklaces.
- Product sections: Close details, Specifications, On scale, Care and details, Delivery and pickup, Reviews, Complete the set, Recently viewed, and Engrave or gift.
- Complementary and related product recommendations.
- Reviews from your review app, with ratings on product cards.
- Quick view and a variant selector sheet for phones.

### Collections and search

- Filters from the Shopify Search & Discovery app on collection and search pages, with swatch, checkbox and price range styles.
- Sorting, quick filter chips, a filters drawer and load more or numbered pages.
- Merchandising tiles in the product grid.
- Predictive search with products, collections, articles and pages, popular searches and a featured product.

### Cart

- Bag drawer or bag page.
- Order notes, discount codes, item and order discounts, subscription plan names, shipping estimates and a tax note.
- Accelerated checkout buttons in the bag drawer and on the bag page.
- Recommendations in the bag.

### Store-wide

- Mega menu with style, metal, stone and help columns.
- Announcement bar with rotating messages and a daily delivery cut-off message.
- Country, currency and language selectors.
- Customer account component in the header, and Follow on Shop in the footer.
- Four color schemes, fonts from Shopify's font library, and two section spacing options.
- Accessible markup: keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen reader labels and reduced motion support.
- Structured data for search engines and social sharing images.

# Gift card page

When a customer receives a Shopify gift card, the email links to the gift card page. The page shows your logo (or store name), the gift card's value, its code with a copy button, a QR code that can be scanned in your physical store, and a button to add the card to Apple Wallet.

To sell gift cards, create a gift card product in your Shopify admin under **Products > Gift cards**. Customers can send a gift card to someone else from the product page. See [Gift card products](product-pages.md#gift-card-products).

To edit the page, choose **Others > Gift card** in the theme editor's page selector.

## Gift card section

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Color scheme** | The colors of the page. |
| **Headline** | The heading, such as "Your gift card." |
| **QR code** | Shows the QR code. |
| **Show Apple Wallet button** | Shows the Add to Apple Wallet button. <!-- verify after merge: currently "Add to wallet" --> |
| **Use label** | The button that takes the customer to your store, such as "Shop now." |
| **Copy label** and **Copied label** | The copy button's text, before and after it's pressed. |

Blocks:

- **Value** (1): **Show balance**, **Expiry** (shows the expiry date, if the card has one) and a **Note**.
- **Instructions** (1): **Text**, **Heading** and **Image**, explaining how to use the card. <!-- verify after merge: "Title" becomes "Heading" -->
- **Support** (up to 2): an **Icon**, **Text** and **Link**, such as "Questions? Write to us."

Disabled and expired gift cards show a message instead of the code.
